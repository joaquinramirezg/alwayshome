## Numerical parameter approval record

The two dollars-per-square-foot observations S3 and S5 are **context only**.
No demolition USD/ft², architect percentage, permit percentage, quality multiplier,
ZIP factor, age multiplier, contingency percentage, inflation forecast or disaster
surge percentage has been verified and approved. Leave all such parameters null.

A later approved benchmark must record: source ID and exact page/table or quote
reference; publication and price-base dates; retrieval date; geographic boundary;
property type/quality/stories/size band; area denominator; currency; cost inclusions
and exclusions; contractor overhead/profit and tax treatment; disaster conditions;
sample and uncertainty if available; license/reuse permission; approver and expiry.
A URL alone is not sufficient evidence for a numerical parameter.
